Selling

Do I have to sign an exclusive contract?

No. Exclusive is never mandatory: you always choose. I'll explain the advantages so you can decide. With an exclusive, your home appears with one price and one beautiful presentation, I can invest in professional photos and real marketing, and you have one contact with a weekly update. And exclusive doesn't mean fewer buyers: I share my listings with other agencies, so their buyers can visit too.

Do you only sell luxury homes?

No. Engel & Völkers has a premium image, but premium is about the service, not the price. I work with all kinds of homes, from studios to large family apartments, as long as I believe I can sell them well for you.

Who decides the price?

You do, always. I look at recent sales and current listings of similar homes nearby, and at your home's condition, floor, light and outdoor space. I explain everything in a written market study, so you can choose the asking price with real facts. The same goes for offers: I advise you, but you decide the final sale price.

What do you need from me to get started?

For your free market study, your caderneta predial, your energy certificate if possible, and a short visit to your home. Before your home goes online, I need all the documents: caderneta predial, certidão permanente, energy certificate, and the ID and NIF of every owner. I help you request anything that's missing. Once everything is complete, we sign the mediation contract, and the photo shoot follows the same or the next day.

Why do you ask for so many documents?

Because the law requires it. In Portugal, every real estate agent must identify both the seller and the buyer and complete an anti-money laundering form. This is mandatory for every sale. I'll ask for your ID (Cartão de Cidadão or passport) and NIF, and for buyers also where the funds come from. Checking everything early protects you and avoids problems later, at the promissory contract or the deed.

Do I need an energy certificate?

Yes. A valid certificado energético is required to advertise a property in Portugal. If you don't have one, I can put you in touch with a certified expert.

When do I pay your commission?

Only when your home is sold. The commission is agreed upfront in the mediation contract and is paid half at the CPCV and half at the escritura.

How long does a sale take?

Finding the right buyer depends on price, condition and demand. With the right price and a complete file, homes in and around Estrela usually find a buyer within a few months. After that, everything goes quickly in Portugal: the promissory contract (CPCV) is usually signed within one to three weeks, and the deed often follows one to two months later. That's why I prepare your documents from the start, so nothing slows the sale down.

Buying

How do I prepare financially to buy?

Know your total budget, including IMT, stamp duty, deed and registration fees on top of the price. If you need a mortgage, get a pre-approval before you visit; non-residents usually need a larger down payment. I help you prepare and connect you with trusted mortgage brokers, so contact me early.

Can I buy from abroad?

Yes. You need a Portuguese tax number (NIF) and a Portuguese bank account. I can introduce you to a trusted lawyer who helps with both and can even sign for you with a power of attorney, so you don't need to fly in for every step.

What do T1, T2 and T3 mean?

In Portugal, homes are described by their number of bedrooms. A T0 is a studio, a T1 has one bedroom, a T2 two, and so on. A T2+1 has an extra room that doesn't officially count as a bedroom, for example because it has no window. It's often used as an office or a walk-in closet.

How long does buying take?

Faster than in many other countries, and faster than most people expect. My own purchase went very quickly too. Once your offer is accepted, the promissory contract (CPCV) is usually signed within one to three weeks, with a deposit of at least 10% of the price, sometimes more. The deed often follows one to two months later. A cash purchase can be done in about four to six weeks in total; with a mortgage, count on two to three months. That's why I help you prepare your documents and financing before you make an offer.

Looking for a home outside Lisbon?

My focus is Estrela and the neighbourhoods around it. For a small number of buyers I also search myself in Costa da Caparica, Arrábida & Sesimbra, Comporta and the Alentejo. Beyond Lisbon → Looking on the Silver Coast, in Cascais, the Algarve or Porto? I'll introduce you to a trusted Engel & Völkers colleague there.

Mortgage

How much can I borrow?

For your own home, Portuguese banks usually lend up to 90% of the value, for a second home or an investment up to 80%. So you need savings for the rest of the price, plus the purchase taxes and fees. How much exactly depends on your income: a broker can tell you in one conversation.

Can I get a mortgage if I don't live in Portugal?

Yes, several Portuguese banks lend to non-residents. They usually ask for a bigger down payment and more documents, so start early, before you make an offer.

Why get a pre-approval before visiting?

You know exactly how much you can spend, and sellers take your offer seriously.

Does the mortgage broker cost me anything?

No. The broker I work with compares several banks for you, at no cost to you. Ask me and I'll introduce you personally.
