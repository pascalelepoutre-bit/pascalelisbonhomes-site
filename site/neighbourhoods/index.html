My focus area

Estrela

Green, calm and classic. Life here turns around the Jardim da Estrela and the white dome of the basilica, with tram 28 rattling past. Buyers come for the elegant early-20th-century buildings, high ceilings and wooden floors, and for a real neighbourhood feel close to the centre.

Lapa

Lisbon's embassy quarter and one of its most prestigious addresses. Quiet, sloping streets with palacetes, gardens behind high walls and, from the upper floors, wide views over the Tagus. Homes here are rare on the market and buyers are often ready to wait for the right one.

Santos

Between Lapa and the river, Santos mixes design shops and studios with quiet residential streets. Away from the busiest tourist areas, but close to the water, the train to Cascais and the bridge to the south bank.

A village inside the city. Straight, flat streets, local shops, cafés and one of Lisbon's favourite markets. Families love it for its everyday convenience and its strong community feel.

Ajuda

On the hill above Belém, Ajuda is quiet and residential, with the Palácio da Ajuda and one of Lisbon's oldest botanical gardens. A real local feel, close to the river and to Monsanto park.
