Why I like it

A new building on Avenida 24 de Julho, a few minutes' walk from Santos and Madragoa. Every apartment is delivered turnkey, with an interior design by Varino: you move in without works or choices to make. Many homes have balconies or terraces, several with views of the river, and the top floors have private rooftops.

Around the corner: Jardim de Santos, the Museu de Arte Antiga and the river. Santos station takes you to the Cascais beaches, and the centre is ten minutes away.

At a glance

  • A show apartment is ready to visit today
