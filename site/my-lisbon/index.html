Sunrise, by the river…
Run

From my home to Belém and back

I regularly run along the river at sunrise, from my home to Belém and back. The light on the water and the bridge is magic. And a little secret most people don't know: you can run laps around the port, between the boats and the containers.

On the last Sunday of every month you'll find me at a living room concert, in someone's home and often right here in the neighbourhood. I discover new places and new people, and every time I fall in love with Lisbon, and with life, all over again.
