Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026. I keep this short and clear: what I collect, why, and what you can ask me at any time.

Who I am

This website belongs to Pascale Lepoutre, real estate consultant working with EV MMC Portugal – Mediação Imobiliária, Unipessoal Lda., Engel & Völkers Lisboa, AMI 15208. For any question about your data, write to pascale.lepoutre@engelvoelkers.com.

What I collect

Only what you send me yourself through the forms or by WhatsApp: your name, phone number, email, the address or street of your property, what you are looking for, such as budget, areas and timing, and any documents you choose to upload, such as your caderneta predial or energy certificate.

Why I use it

  • To answer your question and prepare your free market study.
  • To search for homes that fit what you are looking for.
  • To send you occasional updates, only if you subscribed. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To introduce you to my credit partner ComparaJá, only if you ticked the box to agree.

I use your data because you asked me to contact you, to prepare a mediation contract with you, or because you gave your consent.

Who sees it

  • Engel & Völkers Lisboa, the agency I work with, where your details are recorded in our client system.
  • ComparaJá, only with your consent, for financing.
  • A trusted colleague in another region of Portugal, only with your consent, when you ask me to connect you.
  • The services that run this website and its forms, Netlify, and WhatsApp when you message me there.

I never sell your data. When a sale or purchase goes ahead, Portuguese law requires me to identify sellers and buyers, and that information is kept as the law requires.

How long I keep it

As long as we are in contact about selling or buying, and up to two years after our last contact, unless the law requires longer. Newsletter subscribers stay on the list until they unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time to see, correct or delete your data, to stop using it, or to withdraw your consent. Just send me an email. You can also file a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD, at www.cnpd.pt.

Cookies and visitor statistics

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to show them. To understand how many people visit and which pages they read, I use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It counts visits without cookies and without collecting personal data, so it cannot identify you. To save you typing, your name, phone number and email are remembered only in your own browser, on your device, so the other forms are filled in for you. Nothing is sent until you press send.
